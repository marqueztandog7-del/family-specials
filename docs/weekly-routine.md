# The weekly routine (the product brief)

Every feature should make this routine faster or cheaper. (Saved 16 Oct 2026; updated 16 Oct 2026 for the
Home dashboard and phone notifications.)

- **Wednesday:** the Woolworths and other updates run. A phone notification arrives when a watched item goes on
  special (at most one per item per week), plus the weekly digest. The bell on Home counts new alerts.
- **Wednesday night, about 10 minutes:** open the app on **Home**. Potential savings this week tells us if it's
  worth a look. "New on special this week" and "Recommended to add" each add to the list in one tap.
  **This Week** shows everything on special and where it's cheapest. Then decide where to shop: everything at one
  store, or split across stores to save more.
- **At the shops:** tick items off the **List** as we buy them, even with poor reception.
- **Afterwards:** **Savings** shows what we saved, and price history keeps building.

The app's layout: five tabs, **This Week · Watchlist · Home · List · Savings**, opening on Home (in the middle).
Alerts sit behind the bell on Home, and Settings behind the gear on every screen. Details are in
[app-plan.md](app-plan.md).
