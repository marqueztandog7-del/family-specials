# The weekly routine (the product brief)

Every feature should make this routine faster or cheaper. (Saved 16 Oct 2026.)

- **Wednesday:** the Woolworths and other updates run, and the app flags any watched item that's gone on special.
- **Wednesday night, about 10 minutes:** open the app, see what's on special from our list and where it's cheapest, add items to the shopping list in one tap, and decide where to shop: everything at one store, or split across stores to save more.
- **At the shops:** tick items off the list as we buy them, even with poor reception.
- **Afterwards:** the savings tracker shows what we saved, and price history keeps building.
